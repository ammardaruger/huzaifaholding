Business Divisions

01 / REAL ESTATE

Real Estate

Residential, commercial and industrial properties defined by cost-effectiveness, innovative design and sustainability.

02 / TRANSPORTATION

Transportation

Logistics and heavy transport services — flatbed logistics, dumper truck operations, heavy equipment rental and tanker transport across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

03 / CONSTRUCTION

Construction

Engineering, procurement and construction across civil infrastructure, electromechanical works and site facilities, serving oil & gas, power, healthcare and industrial sectors through Turnkey, Unit Rate and cost-plus contracts.

04 / MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing

High-quality products for diverse markets, built on excellence, precision and innovation.

05 / CHEMICALS

Chemicals

06 / TRADING

Trading

Connecting markets and facilitating the flow of goods through a global network and market knowledge.

07 / FINE DINING

Fine Dining

Culinary experiences built on exceptional service, quality ingredients and memorable moments.

08 / ULTRA LUXURY
